Test Description:

A large portion of resistant patients have acquired point mutations in the

ABL1 kinase domain that renders the kinase resistant to the drug. Sites of

point mutations in ABL1 associated with imatinib resistance span the entire

kinase domain but often cluster in important hotspots.(1,2)

Total RNA was extracted and nested reverse transcription PCR was

performed to detect the BCR-ABL1 transcript and ABL1 kinase domain (KD)

region was thereafter sequenced by Next Generation Sequencing for

detection of mutant alleles.

Limitations:

-

Please note that only the variants relevant to the patient’s clinical presentation were reported. A report on all variants present is available

upon request, please contact the laboratory.

-

Predisposition to disease may be affected by non genetic factors.

-

This test has not been approved by external accrediting bodies and has been validated according to internal laboratory criteria. Accreditation is

not required for the reporting of these test results.

-

For Accurate detection of mutation, at least 1000 BCR-ABL1 Fusion copies/ml should be present in the sample.

-

In cases where the BCR ABL1 load is very low, cases may be released as ‘No Mutation Detected’ with a comment ‘Repeated sequencing revealed

no sequenceable Amplicons due to low transcript levels’

-

All laboratory tests are associated with an error rate of ~1%. These could be due to sample mismatch, inappropriate labeling, processing or

technological limitations. Please correlate with clinical features and other investigations for final conclusion and send a repeat sample for

analysis if necessary.

-

Rare false positive or negative results may occur due to inherent limitations of the technique involved.
